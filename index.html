Tell us what you have in mind

At home

We deliver the board ready to serve and set it up where you want it. Tell us about the table, the number of guests, and the time you plan to start eating. We time the build so the cheese comes to the right temperature and the crackers stay crisp.

Dietary needs go on the board plan from the beginning. Vegetarian, gluten-free, nut-free, and dairy-free boards are all part of the regular menu.

On the water

A boat board is built differently. Lower sides keep things in place in a wake. Firm cheeses and cured meats hold up in the sun better than soft ones. Skewers, picks, and glass stay at the dock.

Send us the marina, the slip, and your departure time. We hand the board off at the dock with ice packs sized for your cooler and a short card on how long things can sit out.

How it works

  1. Tell us the basics

    Date, number of guests, home or boat, and any allergies or things your group won't eat.

  2. Get a menu and a quote

    We send a board plan with what goes on it and what it costs. Change anything you like.

  3. Hold the date

    A deposit reserves your day. We buy fresh the day before or the morning of.

  4. We deliver and set up

    At your door or at the dock, ready to serve, with notes on how to keep it at its best.
